What Does "Pono" Mean? ⚖️🌺

Hawaiian for Righteousness, Moral Balance, Harmony & Doing What Is Right

Quick Answer

"Pono" (pronounced "POH-noh") is one of the deepest guiding values in Hawaiian culture, meaning righteousness, moral integrity, fairness, natural balance, and doing what is right. Whether etched into Hawaii's state motto or spoken in everyday Pidgin ("Gotta do da pono ting, brah"), pono describes the harmony you feel when your actions align with respect for people, community, and ka ʻāina.

How to Pronounce "Pono"

POH-noh

Syllable Breakdown:

  • "PO-" (POH) — primary stress, round "oh" vowel (never flat "pah-noh").
  • "-no" (noh) — clean matching "noh" syllable.

How Pono Shapes Hawaiian Language & Daily Island Life

In the classic Hawaiian Dictionary by Pukui & Elbert, the entry for pono runs longer than almost any other moral term because it spans ethics, health, craftsmanship, and cosmic order:

Phrase / Word Meaning & Context
Live Pono / Stay Pono Everyday Hawaii reminder to act honestly, share generously, respect elders, and leave places better than you found them.
Hoʻoponopono Hoʻo- ("to make") + ponopono = "to set right" — traditional Hawaiian family conflict resolution, truth-telling, and forgiveness.
Ua Mau ke Ea o ka ʻĀina i ka Pono Spoken by King Kamehameha III at Thomas Square (Honolulu, 1843): "The life/sovereignty of the land is perpetuated in righteousness."
Pono vs. Kapakahi When things are balanced, fair, and proper they are pono; when something is crooked, one-sided, or out of whack, locals call it kapakahi!

Real Sentence Examples in Hawaii

"You gotta do da pono ting, yeah — return da wallet wit' all da cash inside."

Translation: "You have to do the right thing — return the wallet with all the cash still inside."

"Take only what your ʻohana going eat — dat's pono fishing."

Translation: "Harvest only what your family will eat — that's responsible, balanced fishing."

"Tutu folks taught us sit down make hoʻoponopono before da sun go down."

Translation: "Our grandparents taught us to sit down and make things right with each other before sunset."

"Drive safe home tonight, bruddah — stay pono!"

Translation: "Drive home safely tonight, brother — stay well and walk right!"

Pono Meaning FAQ

What does pono mean in Hawaiian?

Pono (pronounced POH-noh) means righteousness, integrity, balance, harmony, and doing what is morally right toward people and the land.

How do you pronounce pono?

Say POH-noh with two pure "oh" vowels and stress on the first syllable.

What does "Live Pono" or "Stay Pono" mean in Hawaii?

It means living with honesty, humility, balance, and respect — making right choices even when nobody is watching.

How does pono appear in Hawaii's State Motto?

Hawaii's motto is "Ua Mau ke Ea o ka ʻĀina i ka Pono" — "The life of the land is perpetuated in righteousness," spoken by King Kamehameha III in 1843.

How is pono related to hoʻoponopono?

Hoʻoponopono ("to make doubly right") is built directly from pono and refers to the Hawaiian family practice of reconciliation and restoring harmony.
